Before you ask

What we don't promise

  • No ranking guarantee. Nobody controls what a language model says. We move the inputs that are movable and report what happened, including when it's less than we hoped.
  • No claim about patient volume. AI answers generate no referral traffic, so honest attribution from visibility to bookings isn't currently possible. Anyone selling you that number should be asked how they got it.
  • No medical claims. Every line we write passes a Texas-specific claims filter — no outcome language, no "permanent," correct good-faith-exam and medical-director wording. We'll decline a request to write otherwise.
  • No scraped chat interfaces. Official APIs only, method printed in every report.
  • No patient data. We never ask for, receive, or store anything from your EHR or scheduling system.
